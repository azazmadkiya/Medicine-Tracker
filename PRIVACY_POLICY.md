# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Medicine Tracker**. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information and data when you use our Android application.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
**Medicine Tracker is an offline-first application.** We deeply respect your privacy, especially regarding your health and medical data. 
* **Local Storage:** All data you enter into the app—including medication schedules, inventory, appointment details, and healthcare contacts—is stored entirely **locally on your device** using an internal database.
* **No Cloud Syncing:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or process your personal or health data on any external servers. 
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or provide personal identifiable information (PII) to us to use the app.

## 3. Device Permissions
The app may request certain device permissions (such as Notifications) solely to provide core functionalities, like alerting you when it is time to take your medication. We do not use these permissions to collect data for our own purposes.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Because the app operates locally, we do not share your data with third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services. 

## 5. Security
The security of your medical data is important to us. Since your data is stored locally on your device, we recommend securing your device with a PIN, fingerprint, or password to protect your information from unauthorized physical access.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository or the contact information provided on the Google Play Store page.
